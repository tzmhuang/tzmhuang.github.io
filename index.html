Zanming Huang

I am a PhD Student at [The Ohio State Univerisity](https://cse.osu.edu/), advised by [Prof. Wei-lun (Harry) Chao](https://sites.google.com/view/wei-lun-harry-chao/home). I had the previledge to work with [Prof. Yu Su](https://ysu1989.github.io/) as well as [Prof. Eshed Ohn-Bar](https://eshed1.github.io). My research focuses on enabling intelligent systems to perceive, reason, and plan across diverse environments and embodiments.
